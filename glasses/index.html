How it works

  • This works with timers at beta.timed-show.com.
  • The timer’s operator chooses whether these glasses are a presenter’s or the operator’s, and can change it at any time (share → the device list).
  • Presenter: the time left, small and dim, in a corner. It gets brighter in the last minute and when time is up (shown as + and the time over), and says paused while the timer is paused. Tap the glasses to hide or show it. Choose the corner here, or on the glasses: tap, then tap and hold to open the menu, and choose Next corner.
  • Operator: the operator’s screen in miniature: elapsed and total along the top, the message on screen and the countdown in the middle, started and ends-at times along the bottom. Tap three times quickly to go to the next item in the run sheet (fewer taps do nothing).
  • Before pairing, the glasses say Timer not connected. If the timer closes they say Timer offline and reconnect when it reopens. The pairing is remembered.
  • To close the app, tap, then tap and hold on the glasses (Even’s menu) and choose close.
  • Unpair forgets the timer. The timer’s operator can also take the glasses’ access away at any time (share → the device list).
