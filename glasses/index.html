Timed Show

The presentation timer’s countdown in the corner of your glasses.

Pair with a timer

On the timer, click share, then Pair a computer (on a phone or tablet: ⋯ → Let a friend control this timer). Type the 6-digit code it shows:

Ask the timer’s operator to type this number in the timer’s share panel:

It’s shown here only, not on your glasses. One wrong number and the timer refuses the glasses.

How it works

  • This works with timers at beta.timed-show.com.
  • The glasses show the time left, small and dim. It gets brighter in the last minute and when time is up (shown as + and the time over), and says paused while the timer is paused.
  • Before pairing, the glasses say Timer not connected. If the timer closes they say Timer offline and reconnect when it reopens. The pairing is remembered.
  • To close the app, press and hold on the glasses and choose close.
  • Unpair forgets the timer. The timer’s operator can also take the glasses’ access away at any time (share → the device list).
