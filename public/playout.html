    Stream Deck

    A key for each item with cues. With Follow the timer off, pressPress one to start its item (as ▶ Start item). On an item that’s playing, a tap stops it and holding the key fades it out over s (let go to stop it there). The last key does the same for everything; ◀ ▶ turn the page. With Follow on, the keys show the run sheet and only stop or fade. : a key that starts something while others play fades them out over s from the moment you let it go. Extra keys: drop audio or video files from your computer onto an empty key below (several fill the keys after it), or choose them with Add files to keys… (the first empty keys), for anything that isn’t on the run sheet: they play when pressed, Follow or not. + Page adds a page of empty keys.

      Options
      Off: while an item runs from ▶ Start item, its own cues’ ▶ and ■ are locked (■ Stop item stops them). On: for rehearsals, every cue stays in your hands.
      Levels Each output plays every cue as loud as its quietest one, so no cue is louder than the next: the louder ones are turned down to it (measured as they play, in LUFS; nothing is ever turned up). Off: every cue as recorded (a ✂ trim still applies). The level box (top right) shows it: set the desk (▶ Tone helps), then click the box; it flashes when the level changes. Console trim = the LUFS trim offset − the level: set the offset once so it reads what your desk's digital trim sounds right at, and from then on set the desk's trim to it when the level changes. No trim on the channel? A compressor's makeup gain does the same, with the compressor itself out of the way (a 1:1 ratio, or a threshold it never reaches).
