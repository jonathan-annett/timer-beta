Run sheet

    Random stings

    A walk-up sting at the start of each item long enough to be a speaker, picked at random, none used twice, and copied to this computer like any cue. Items with a start cue of your own are left alone; 🎲 on an item picks another.

    Stream Deck

    A key for each item with cues. With Follow the timer off, press one to start its item (as ▶ Start item). On an item that’s playing, a tap stops it and holding the key fades it out over s (let go to stop it there). The last key does the same for everything; ◀ ▶ turn the page. With Follow on, the keys show the run sheet and only stop or fade.

    Output
    Video cues play here
    External playback activatedVideos play in the floating window: drag it to the other screen and double-click it for full screen.
    Sound outputs

    Cues play to the show output; the ▶ previews and the clip editor to the other, so you can listen on headphones during the show.

    Options
    Off: while an item runs from ▶ Start item, its own cues’ ▶ and ■ are locked (■ Stop item stops them). On: for rehearsals, every cue stays in your hands.
    Levels Each output turns its cues down to its own loudness, so one isn’t louder than the next: measured as they play, in LUFS (−23 is broadcast’s level, −14 a music service’s). Never turned up: a quieter cue plays as recorded. Off: every cue as recorded (a ✂ trim still applies). A change applies at once, to what’s playing too.
